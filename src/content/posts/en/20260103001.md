# Migrating the Old Blog (Plan Revised)

The Typecho and WordPress (Zibll) sites I used to run were paused in February 2025 for reasons of their own. Here is the tally of what had been published on them:

Typecho: 11 posts

WordPress: 14 posts

Technical: 20 posts

Experience / write-ups: 2 posts

Software and event sharing: 3 posts

On 30 January 2026 the plan was revised: sync one or two posts per day where possible.

## Conventions for migrated posts

- Every migrated post is tagged **and** categorised as **"旧内容" (Legacy)**;
- Every migrated post opens with a **notice that its content may be out of date**;

- Every migrated post **keeps the publication date of the original**.
